About

The numbers,and the peoplebehind them.

B.S. Finance · Penn State · Smeal College of Business · Class of 2027

I’m a finance student at Penn State Smeal who is most at home with the questions that don’t have an easy answer.

I’ve always been the person who asks one more “why.” Why does this number look the way it does? Who made the decision behind it? What would change if we looked at it differently? To me, finance isn’t just models and margins. It’s a way of understanding how people make choices, and how to help them make better ones.

I’m curious by nature and a builder by habit. When something doesn’t make sense, I take it apart until it does. When the tool I need doesn’t exist, I try to create it.
