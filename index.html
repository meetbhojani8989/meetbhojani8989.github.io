About

The numbers, and the people behind them.

I’m a finance student at Penn State Smeal who is most at home with the questions that don’t have an easy answer. I’ve always been the person who asks one more why. To me, finance isn’t just models and margins. It’s a way of understanding how people make choices, and how to help them make better ones.

I’m curious by nature and a builder by habit. When something doesn’t make sense, I take it apart until it does. When the tool I need doesn’t exist, I build it. I’m happiest where finance and technology meet, and where something complicated finally becomes clear.

The people I work with would probably describe me as calm, dependable and quick to pick things up. I take ownership of what’s in front of me, I’m honest about what I don’t know, and I’m looking for a team that cares about getting things right, and a chance to grow alongside them.
