About

The numbers, and the people behind them.

B.S. Finance · Penn State · Smeal College of Business · Class of 2027

I’m a finance student at Penn State Smeal who is most at home with the questions that don’t have an easy answer.

I’ve always been the person who asks one more “why.” Why does this number look the way it does? Who made the decision behind it? What would change if we looked at it differently? To me, finance isn’t just models and margins. It’s a way of understanding how people make choices, and how to help them make better ones.

I’m curious by nature and a builder by habit. When something doesn’t make sense, I take it apart until it does. When the tool I need doesn’t exist, I try to create it. I’d rather learn something hard than do something easy, and I’m comfortable being the least experienced person in the room, because that’s usually where I learn the most.

The people I work with would probably describe me as calm, dependable and quick to pick things up. I take ownership of what’s in front of me, I’m honest about what I don’t know, and I care about doing the work well, not just getting it done.

I’m happiest where finance and technology meet, and where something complicated finally becomes clear. Most of all, I’m looking for a team that cares about getting things right, and a chance to grow alongside them.
