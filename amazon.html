The insight

In the 2025 base year AWS is about 17% of revenue but 55% of operating income ($32.3B of $58.8B). Growing at 18% against retail’s 6%, AWS reaches roughly 68% of operating income by 2030, so the valuation is really a bet on the cloud business.
