How a signal is made

  1. PullA year of daily closing prices for the stock.
  2. Measure1-year return, annualized volatility, Sharpe ratio (4% risk-free) and maximum drawdown.
  3. ScoreFinal score = 55% return score + 45% risk score, out of 5.
  4. DecidePasses, Watch or Weak screen, plus the first risk limit it breaks.
